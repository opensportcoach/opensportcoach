# A 30-disc "regression" that never happened

After 12 days without legs, his leg press read 150 → 120 and hip thrust 62.5 → 40. Both looked like
a serious regression. **Neither was.** He had used the classic machines rather than the Biostrength,
because the gym was packed.

## The diagnostic worth keeping

**The drop sorted by LOADING MECHANISM, not by effort.** Every selectorized-stack lift hit its
prescription exactly and earned a bump — lying curl, seated curl, seated calf. Only the two
disc-loaded lifts fell. **Fatigue does not discriminate by how a machine is loaded. Equipment does.**

That pattern is what made it findable, and it needed breadth across ten exercises at once.

## Why I asked instead of concluding

The 2026-08-14 "machine increment" hypothesis about the lever row and seated cable row was **wrong**,
and he disproved it on 08-19 by hitting both. Having been wrong once about equipment, the second
time was worth a question.

See [[per-machine-disc-lifts]] for how it is prescribed now.
