# Code of conduct

OpenSportCoach is built by people who train, for people who train — at every level, every age, every
body and every background. Everyone is welcome here.

**Expected:** be respectful and patient; assume good intent; give and accept constructive feedback;
credit others' work; keep discussions on the project.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, discrimination, sexualised language or imagery, personal
attacks, body-shaming, publishing anyone's private information (including their training or health
data), and any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

**Health topics:** this is not a medical forum. Don't give medical advice to others; point them to a
professional.

**Enforcement:** maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues or contributions that break this
code, and may block people who repeat it. Report a problem privately to the maintainers through the
repository's private reporting channel (see [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md)); reports are handled
confidentially.

This code applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews.
